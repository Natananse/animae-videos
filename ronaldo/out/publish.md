## YouTube description

Animated tribute, not real footage.

Before the records and the trophies, there was a boy chasing a ball.

This 3:20 animated story follows Cristiano Ronaldo from Madeira through Sporting, Manchester United, Madrid, Juventus in Turin, Al Nassr in Riyadh and Portugal. It connects the effort behind a football action with the meaning of a national-team farewell.

Generic trophy models illustrate United’s 2008 Champions League and Premier League wins, Ronaldo’s first Ballon d’Or and the 2008 FIFA World Player of the Year award; Madrid’s four Champions League titles and four further Ballon d’Or wins; and Portugal’s Euro 2016 and Nations League titles in 2019 and 2025. The cabinet collects only the prizes named in this story.

On 5 November 2026, Ronaldo announced his retirement from international football. The farewell concerns Portugal; his club career continues.

The childhood attempt, mistake and recovery are illustrative dramatization, covered by the opening animation disclaimer. Supporting players, goalkeeper and supporters are generic characters, not portraits of named real players. Their staged actions do not reconstruct every detail of a historical match. The character, uniforms and environments are stylized. They do not recreate a documented private childhood event or use official club crests, logos or sponsors. Narration is original writing delivered by the synthetic voice en-GB-RyanNeural, not a real Ronaldo quotation.
